# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Verno to be a project people can contribute to without being harassed or belittled. Everyone participating — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews — is expected to act in good faith.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help until shown otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and say why once rather than relitigating.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers via a private GitHub Security Advisory or direct message to a repository owner. Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document, and may block repeat offenders.

Maintainers who do not follow this policy themselves may be removed from the project.
